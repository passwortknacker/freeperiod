Round 7 – design direction (read-only concept round, no file changes).

You are the design lead for "FreePeriod." – a free, private, offline Android period tracker
(Kotlin/Compose, Material 3). Read the spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-freeperiod-design.md`
and look at the current screenshots in `app/src/test/screenshots/` (today/, history/, day/). They
are functional but generic Material.

Owner's brief (verbatim): "Please always use the highest design standards, we want a stunning but
simplistic product that is easy and fun to use! Don't just be like any other app though. We want
to incorporate our own unique style that sets us apart without being annoying. Don't always take
the easiest route design wise, add some nice things and some colour, make things lively without
making them cluttered."

Fixed constraints: name "FreePeriod." (the full stop is part of the name – pun: period = full
stop); calm, grown-up, trustworthy, not pink-cliché, not esoteric/moon/astrology; neutral in-app
wording; light + dark; DE + EN; accessibility (contrast AA, never colour alone, large fonts,
TalkBack); no network (fonts/assets must be bundled, OFL/Apache licensed); performance on cheap
phones; no fertility/contraception claims.

Deliver 3 clearly distinct design directions, each:
- Name + one-line idea; the signature motif (what makes it recognisably ours).
- Palette (hex) light + dark incl. accent, surfaces, period colour, predicted colour, 2–3
  supporting colours for liveliness; how colour is used without clutter.
- Typography (specific bundled fonts + where used, e.g. display numerals).
- Concrete treatment of: Today card (cycle visual instead of/with the cycle-day number), month
  calendar markers, day entry (chips/icons/mood faces, categories), history (cycle chart),
  onboarding first screen, empty states, launcher icon idea.
- Motion & delight: 3–5 micro-interactions/haptics (e.g. on "Period started"), with restraint.
- Voice: 3 example microcopy lines EN + DE.
- Risks (what could become annoying/cluttered) and how to keep it simple.
- Implementation cost in Compose (low/medium/high) and anything that needs new dependencies.

Then recommend one direction (or a blend) with reasons, and list the 5 highest-impact changes to
make first. Keep it concrete enough that a developer can implement it without guessing.

Answer: English Markdown, max ~1800 words. No code, no file changes.
